Meet the artist

The face and hands behind Vanity Affairs Luxe Studio.

Salma Sami Khan

Certified makeup artist for brides, film and the runway

I'm Salma Sami Khan, the founder of Vanity Affairs Luxe Studio. I've been working as a professional makeup artist since 2008, creating looks for brides, special events, fashion shows, film and television.

Over the years I have learned that great makeup is equal parts technique and trust. I listen carefully, plan every look around your features and the occasion, and work calmly, even on the busiest wedding morning or film set.

Having worked with clients from many cultures and backgrounds, I'm comfortable with a wide range of skin tones, traditions and styles, from soft, natural looks to full glamour.

  • Certified makeup artist: bridal, film and runway
  • Serving clients across Canada since 2015
  • Based in Toronto, available for travel across the GTA
